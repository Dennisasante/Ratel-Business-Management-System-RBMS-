# Adding Ratel to your home screen

No App Store or Play Store needed — this installs straight from the browser
and opens full-screen like a normal app from then on.

## iPhone / iPad (Safari)

1. Open **your Ratel web address** in **Safari** (must be Safari — Chrome on
   iOS can't do this).
2. Tap the **Share** icon (square with an arrow pointing up), in the bottom
   toolbar (iPhone) or top-right (iPad).
3. Scroll down and tap **Add to Home Screen**.
4. Tap **Add** in the top-right corner.
5. A Ratel icon now appears on your home screen — tap it any time to open
   Ratel full-screen, no browser address bar.

## Android (Chrome)

1. Open **your Ratel web address** in **Chrome**.
2. Tap the **⋮** menu (top-right).
3. Tap **Add to Home screen** (sometimes shown as **Install app**).
4. Confirm by tapping **Add** / **Install**.
5. A Ratel icon now appears on your home screen — tap it any time to open
   Ratel full-screen, no browser address bar.

Chrome may also show an **Install** banner or icon in the address bar on its
own — either route ends the same way.

## Notes

- This needs an internet connection to sign in and work, same as using it in
  a browser tab — nothing about this makes it work offline.
- Each staff member does this once, on their own phone or tablet — it's not
  something you set up centrally.
- To remove it later: press and hold the home screen icon, same as removing
  any other app.
